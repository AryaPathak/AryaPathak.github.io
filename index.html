03 / Playbook

How I run go-to-market

The sequence I ran at Clyro, and the one I'd run again. Each step feeds the next, because messaging is only as good as the research under it.

STEP 01

Market & ICP research

Work out who the product is actually for before writing a word about it. Competitive teardowns, category mapping, and customer conversations that define the ideal customer profile.

ICP definitionCompetitive analysisMarket sizing
STEP 02

Positioning & messaging

Turn research into a defensible position: what the product replaces, why it wins, and the proof behind each claim. One message hierarchy the whole company can use.

Value propsMessage hierarchyProof points
STEP 03

Technical narrative

Docs, blogs, landing pages, and website copy that survive contact with a skeptical engineer. I read the code and the API before I write the page.

API docsLanding pagesWebsite copySEO
STEP 04

Launch

Run the launch with product and engineering: scope the story, write release notes and customer education, sequence the beats, and ship the collateral on time.

Launch strategyRelease notesProduct collateral
STEP 05

Sales enablement

Give the sales team language that holds up on a call: one-pagers, demo narratives, objection handling, and the technical answers that unblock a deal.

One-pagersObjection handlingHubSpot
STEP 06

Distribution & feedback

Treat distribution as a system, not a post. LinkedIn, X, newsletters, and search. Then feed what the market says back into the messaging and start again.

Content distributionKeyword analysisFeedback loops
